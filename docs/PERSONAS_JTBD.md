# Proto-Personas and Jobs to Be Done

**Evidence label:** These are working hypotheses for product discovery, not personas validated through completed interviews.

## Primary — Product Manager at a B2B SaaS company

**Context:** Responsible for understanding customer problems, defining roadmap priorities, and aligning design and engineering.

**Jobs to be done**
- When preparing a roadmap review, I want to identify recurring customer problems so that I can focus the discussion on patterns rather than anecdotes.
- When comparing opportunities, I want reach, impact, confidence, and effort assumptions visible so that trade-offs are explainable.
- When a feature ships, I want a documented success metric so that the team can evaluate whether the original problem improved.

**Potential pain points to validate**
- Feedback arrives in several systems.
- Synthesis depends on manual spreadsheets or individual memory.
- Prioritisation inputs are inconsistently documented.
- Experiment plans are disconnected from the original customer problem.

## Secondary — Product Operations

**Context:** Supports feedback processes, taxonomy, portfolio reporting, and product rituals.

**JTBD:** When feedback arrives from multiple teams, I want consistent metadata and reporting so that product groups can compare signals without rebuilding manual reports.

**Potential pain points to validate:** inconsistent tags, duplicated records, unclear ownership, high reporting effort.

## Secondary — UX Researcher / Product Analyst

**Context:** Synthesises qualitative evidence and helps product teams understand customer behaviour.

**JTBD:** When a theme is proposed, I want to inspect the underlying feedback and segment context so that I can assess whether the interpretation is supported.

**Potential pain points to validate:** loss of raw context, over-generalised themes, weak provenance from insight to decision.

## Discovery questions

- Who owns the process from feedback intake through roadmap decision?
- Which source causes the most manual work?
- What evidence would change a prioritisation decision?
- How does the team distinguish severity from frequency?
- What existing product or process would StratifyAI need to integrate with?
